Our approach

Small steps that carry a lot of weight

Health isn't made or broken by one meal or one decision. It's the result of small, achievable efforts kept up over a long stretch of time. Everything we teach comes back to eight of them.

A tool, not an end result

A GLP-1 can open a window of opportunity. We help you use that window to build habits you can carry forward once the medication is no longer doing the heavy lifting.

A calorie deficit, not a nutrition deficit

Eating less shouldn't mean starving your body of what it needs. We focus on body composition: keeping muscle while you lose fat, the way a sculptor shapes the whole statue instead of chipping off a chunk of marble.

About us

We give this advice for a living. Then we had to take it.

Well on Your Way started with two friends from the emergency department who both lost the weight themselves, and learned that keeping it off is where the real work is.

Rick Sorensen, MD

Emergency physician · Co-founder

Rick spent 14 years as an Air Force physician and more than 25 in emergency medicine. One Christmas he gave his wife an unusual gift: they would both go through Greg's clinic as patients. Each lost about 60 pounds. Being the patient taught him the plan has to account for life after the losing, starting on day one.

Greg Camp, FNP-C

Emergency nurse practitioner · Co-founder

Greg served 10 years in the Army, then drifted slowly from his health once that structure was gone. Over about two years he lost roughly 50 pounds, and the framework he built became St. George Weight Loss and Wellness, the clinic he has run for five years while still working in the ER.

Questions

Before you sign up

Do I have to be on a GLP-1?

No. Foundations and Restart are built for people using a GLP-1. Wellness Transformation teaches the same eight steps for people improving their health without medication.

Do you prescribe or adjust medication?

No. Our courses are education and coaching. Decisions about your medication stay between you and your own prescriber, and we'll help you know what questions to bring them.

How much time does a course take?

Plan on one live group call and one personal check-in call each week, plus short emails and recorded lessons you can watch on your own schedule.

When do courses start?

Our first cohorts begin in January 2027. Exact dates go to the waitlist first.
